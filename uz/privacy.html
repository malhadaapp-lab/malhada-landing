Sorito

Privacy Policy for users in Uzbekistan

Effective: November 7, 2026 (last updated: November 8, 2026)

«Sorito» (the “App”) is a Korean pronunciation and voice practice app. Its developer, Speechon Studio (Doyeon Kim, Republic of Korea; “we”), seeks to comply with the Law of the Republic of Uzbekistan “On Personal Data” and related legislation. This policy applies to users who downloaded the App from the App Store or Google Play in Uzbekistan. The App does not store your personal information on our servers; most data is processed only on your phone. An Uzbek version is available here.

1. Information collected and purposes

You do not need to provide information that identifies you, such as your name, email or phone number, to use the App.

  • Microphone: used to measure pitch, loudness, pronunciation and speech rate. Most analysis happens on your phone. Audio may be sent off the phone for speech recognition only in Pronunciation practice and “Pronunciation + loudness” practice (section 3).
  • How your voice is used: practice recordings are used only to score your pronunciation and turn it into text (speech recognition). They are not used to identify the speaker or to create a voiceprint. Sorito does not store your voice recordings. OpenAI (United States), which performs speech recognition in “Pronunciation + loudness” practice, keeps them for up to 30 days to prevent abuse, then deletes them, and does not use them to train AI models. In Pronunciation practice, speech is recognized by Apple on iPhone and Google on Android. Your voice is not used to identify or recognize you, and we do not collect biometric data (no voiceprints, voice samples or similar are created). Sorito does not receive any right (license) to use your voice.
  • Practice records, settings and saved recordings: stored only on your phone. The App does not send them to our servers.
  • Subscription (Premium) payments: processed by the App Store or Google Play. We do not receive card details. RevenueCat processes an anonymous ID and purchase history to verify and restore purchases.
  • Usage analytics (if you consent): Google Firebase Analytics receives screens opened, exercises used, number and duration of app sessions, device model, OS version, app ID and approximate country derived from your IP address. Voice and measurement results are not sent. Retention: up to 14 months.
  • Ads (free users): Google AdMob receives your IP address, device information and ad interaction data. If you consent, ads may be personalized; if you decline, only non-personalized ads are shown. On iPhone the App does not ask for “tracking permission”, so the advertising ID (IDFA) is not collected.
  • Screen components and weekly phrase: when fonts, libraries and model voices are loaded from jsDelivr, Google Fonts and GitHub Pages, ordinary connection information such as your IP address is transmitted.
  • Entering practice words (free-input translation and meaning): in free input in Pronunciation practice and “Pronunciation + loudness”, to ① suggest Korean words for a word typed in Uzbek and ② show the Uzbek meaning of a word typed in Korean, only the word you typed is sent through our relay server (Google Cloud, Seoul, Republic of Korea) to OpenAI (United States). If the word is found in the App’s word list, it is not sent. The typed word is not stored (the meaning received is stored only on your phone).
  • “Listen” for free-input words: when you play a word without a model recording with “Listen”, iPhone reads it with the phone’s built-in Korean voice and nothing is sent. On Android, or if the phone has no Korean voice, only that Korean word is sent through the relay server to OpenAI (United States) to create audio. The audio is stored only on your phone; we store neither the word nor the audio.
  • Contacting us by email: if you write to sorito.app@gmail.com, your email address and message are used only to reply, kept for 3 years after the reply, then deleted.

2. Consent

When you first open the App, a “Personal data consent” screen appears. It explains that your information (including your voice, for speech recognition) is transferred outside Uzbekistan to servers in the United States and asks for your consent. If you decline, personalized ads and usage analytics are turned off (non-personalized ads are still shown) and you can keep using the App, including speech recognition. You can change your choice at any time in the App under Settings → “Shaxsiy maʼlumotlarni qayta ishlashga rozilik” (Russian screen: “Согласие на обработку персональных данных”; Personal data consent). If you have declined, “Pronunciation + loudness” practice asks for your consent once before any audio is sent to OpenAI.

4. Retention

  • Data on your phone: until you delete it or uninstall the App.
  • Analytics: up to 14 months.
  • Purchase records: for as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal and tax requirements.
  • Email: 3 years after the reply.

5. Your rights

You have the right to view, correct and delete your information and to withdraw your consent. You can turn off the microphone permission at any time in your device settings. Practice records and saved recordings are stored only on your device: delete everything in the App under Settings → “Barcha maʼlumotlarni oʻchirish” (Delete all data), delete saved recordings one by one under Settings → “Saqlangan yozuvlar” (Saved recordings), or uninstall the App. (If iCloud or Google backup is turned on, the operating system may keep a copy of the data in your personal backup; delete it in your device settings. This backup is in your own Apple or Google account, not on Sorito’s servers, and Sorito cannot see its contents.) Send requests about analytics and purchase records to sorito.app@gmail.com. We will reply within 14 days. You may also file a complaint with the authorized state body for personal data of the Republic of Uzbekistan.

6. In case of a data breach

As soon as we learn that your information has been leaked or accessed without authorization, we will promptly notify the persons concerned and the state body specified by law, take measures to reduce harm and record the incident. Because the App does not create user accounts, notices will be delivered in the App and on this page.

7. Children’s privacy

The App does not collect information that identifies you. If a child uses the App, we ask that a parent or guardian supervise.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will let you know through an App update or an in-app notice.
