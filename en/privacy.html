Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

In short

  • You don’t need an account. We don’t ask for your name or email.
  • The tools in the English version analyze your voice on your phone. That audio is not uploaded.
  • Your practice history and saved recordings stay on your phone.
  • We use Google services for analytics and ads, and RevenueCat to confirm purchases.
  • We don’t sell your personal information and we don’t use your voice to identify you.

1. Who we are

Malhada (“the app”) is developed by Speechon Studio (스피치온스튜디오), based in the Republic of Korea. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean Speechon Studio. If you have a question about your privacy, email malhada.app@gmail.com.

2. Your voice and recordings

Tools that stay on your phone

Shadowing, Pitch Check, Pitch Glide, Loudness Meter, Sustain Timer and Speech Pacer use your microphone to measure pitch, loudness and timing. This processing happens on your phone. Audio from these tools is not sent to us or to anyone else.

What is saved

Recordings and guide voices you choose to save, scripts you type, your practice history and your settings are stored on your phone. They remain there until you delete them or uninstall the app. We cannot see them and cannot recover them. To keep copies, use Export (available after you upgrade) to save them to your phone’s storage or another app.

Korean-language features

When the app is set to Korean on a Google Play account in Korea, two additional features send audio off your phone to turn speech into text:

  • Pronunciation practice uses your phone’s speech recognition service (for example, Google’s), which may process the audio on the provider’s servers under the provider’s own terms.
  • Combined practice sends your recording to our server, which forwards it to OpenAI’s speech-to-text service and returns the text. We do not store this audio or the resulting text on our server.

These features are not part of the English version.

No voice identification

We do not use your voice to identify you, and we do not create voiceprints.

Phone backup

If you have turned on Android backup, your phone may include the app’s data in your own Google account backup so it can be restored on a new phone. This backup is managed by Google under your account. We cannot access it.

4. Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment card details. RevenueCat verifies your purchase using an anonymous ID; it does not receive your name or email from us.

5. What we don’t do

  • We don’t require an account or collect your name, email address or phone number. If you email us, we receive the information in your message.
  • We don’t sell your personal information.
  • We don’t use your voice to identify you.

6. How long information is kept

  • Data on your phone: until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Analytics data: for a limited period set in our Firebase settings, then deleted automatically.
  • Purchase records: as long as needed to provide your purchase and to meet legal and tax obligations.
  • Emails you send us: as long as needed to answer you.

7. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Delete everything on your phone: in the app, go to Settings › Delete all data, or uninstall the app.
  • Delete individual recordings: go to Settings › Saved recordings.
  • Ads: reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings.
  • Analytics and purchase records: email malhada.app@gmail.com. Because there are no accounts, we may ask for details such as the purchase date to find your records.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to opt out of targeted advertising. To make a request, email us. We will not treat you differently for making a request.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, email us and we will delete it.

9. International transfers

We are based in the Republic of Korea, and the services listed above may process information in other countries, including the United States. They process it under their own privacy and security commitments.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we will also tell you in the app or in the Google Play release notes.
