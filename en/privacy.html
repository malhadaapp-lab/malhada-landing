Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

In short

  • You don’t need an account. We don’t ask for your name or email.
  • The tools in the English version analyze your voice, and any audio files you import, on your phone. That audio is not uploaded.
  • Your practice history and saved recordings stay on your phone.
  • We use Google services for analytics and ads, and RevenueCat to confirm purchases.
  • We don’t sell your personal information and we don’t use your voice to identify you.

1. Who we are

Sorito (“the app”) is developed by Speechon Studio (registered business name: 스피치온스튜디오 / Speechonstudio), based in the Republic of Korea. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean Speechon Studio. If you have a question about your privacy, email sorito.app@gmail.com.

2. Your voice and recordings

Tools that stay on your phone

Shadowing, Pitch Check, Pitch Glide, Pitch Hold, Loudness Meter, Sustain Timer and Speech Pacer use your microphone to measure pitch, loudness and timing. This processing happens on your phone. Audio from these tools is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Audio files you import

When you use Create guide from file in Shadowing, the audio file you choose is analyzed only on your phone. Only the sentence you save is kept, as a guide on your phone. The file is never uploaded.

What is saved

Recordings and guide voices you choose to save, scripts you type, your practice history and your settings are stored on your phone. They remain there until you delete them or uninstall the app. We cannot see them and cannot recover them. To keep copies, use Export (available after you upgrade) to save them to your phone’s storage or another app.

Korean-language features

Some features are available only when the app is set to Korean on a Korean store account (Google Play or the App Store). Two of them send audio off the phone for speech recognition. These features are not available in the English version and are described in our Korean privacy policy.

No voice identification

We do not use your voice to identify you, and we do not create voiceprints.

Phone backup

If you have turned on Android backup, your phone may include the app’s data in your own Google account backup so it can be restored on a new phone. This backup is managed by Google under your account. We cannot access it.

On iPhone, if iCloud Backup is on, iOS may include the app’s data in your own iCloud backup. This backup is protected by your Apple ID, and we cannot access it.

4. Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play or the App Store, depending on where you downloaded the app. We do not receive your payment card details. RevenueCat verifies your purchase using an anonymous ID; it does not receive your name or email from us.

5. What we don’t do

  • We don’t require an account or collect your name, email address or phone number. If you email us, we receive the information in your message.
  • We don’t sell your personal information.
  • We don’t use your voice to identify you.

6. How long information is kept

  • Data on your phone: until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Analytics data: up to 14 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Purchase records: as long as needed to provide your purchase and to meet legal and tax obligations.
  • Emails you send us: as long as needed to answer you.

7. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Delete everything on your phone: in the app, go to Settings › Delete all data, or uninstall the app. If Android backup is on, also delete the app’s backup in your phone’s settings (usually Settings › Google › Backup) to remove every copy. On iPhone, delete the app’s backup in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage › Backups.
  • Delete individual recordings: go to Settings › Saved recordings.
  • Ads: on Android, reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings. On iPhone, the app does not collect it.
  • Analytics and purchase records: email sorito.app@gmail.com. Because there are no accounts, we may ask for details such as the purchase date to find your records.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to opt out of targeted advertising. To make a request, email us. We will reply within 30 days, and we will not treat you differently for making a request.
