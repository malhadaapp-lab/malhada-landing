TOOLS

Seven tools and a daily routine

Every tool shows your voice on a live graph, so you can see what changes when you speak differently.

Pitch

Shadowing

Record a guide voice, then say it along with the guide and compare the two pitch curves.

Pitch Check

Read a short passage for 15 seconds to see your low, high and average speaking pitch, in Hz and semitones.

Pitch Glide

Set a low and a high target, then glide between them on a live pitch graph.

Pitch Hold

Hear a note, then hold it for 10 seconds. Five notes, one step higher each time.

Loudness, duration and pace

Loudness Meter

Set a target level and see how often you reach it. Readings are relative, not from a calibrated sound meter.

Sustain Timer

Hold one steady sound and see how long it lasts.

Speech Pacer

Set the beat speed and steps, then speak along with the beat by syllable, word or phrase.

Every day

My routine

Pick the tools you like, put them in order, and run them one after another with one tap.

History

A calendar, a streak and result graphs show how your practice adds up.

PRICE

Free to start

Every tool is free to try, with daily limits on some tools and ads. Full unlock is a one-time purchase through your app store that removes the daily limits and ads. It is not a subscription.

PRIVACY

Analyzed on your phone

In the English version, your voice is analyzed on your phone and isn't uploaded. Your recordings and history are saved only on your phone. Read the privacy policy for details.

Sorito is a practice tool, not a medical device. Keep your voice comfortable and stop if anything hurts.
