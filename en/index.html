Malhada: Voice Pitch Analyzer

See the pitch, loudness and pace of your voice in real time, with simple tools for everyday speaking practice.

Get it on Google Play

GuideYour voice

Six tools

Pitch

Shadowing
Record a guide voice, then say it along with the guide and compare the two pitch curves.
Pitch Check
Read a short passage for 15 seconds to see your low, high and average speaking pitch, in Hz and semitones.
Pitch Glide
Set a low and a high target, then glide between them on a live pitch graph.

Loudness and duration

Loudness Meter
Set a target level and see how often you reach it. Readings are relative, not from a calibrated sound meter.
Sustain Timer
Hold one steady sound and see how long it lasts.

Pace

Speech Pacer
Set the beat speed and steps, then speak along with the beat by syllable, word or phrase.

Free to start

Every tool is free to try, with daily limits on some tools and ads. Full unlock is a one-time purchase through Google Play that removes the daily limits and ads. It is not a subscription.

Your voice stays on your phone

These six tools analyze your voice on your phone. The audio isn’t uploaded, and your recordings and history are saved only on your phone. Read the privacy policy for details.

Malhada is a practice tool, not a medical device. Keep your voice comfortable and stop if anything hurts.
